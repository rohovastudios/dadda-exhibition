About dadda?

dadda? explores the emotional and social realities of single fatherhood through photography, film, installation, personal testimony, and related forms.

Submissions may include private or sensitive experiences involving fatherhood, family relationships, separation, divorce, loss, identity, disability, physical or mental health, trauma, or other personal subjects.

dadda? and Rohova Studios are not mental health organizations, healthcare providers, counseling services, crisis services, support lines, or emergency-response services.

Nothing provided through the website, project, exhibition, or project communications constitutes medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, or other professional advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Submissions and project communications are not continuously monitored. Do not use dadda? to request urgent assistance. If you or another person may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource in your country.
