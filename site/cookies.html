Cookie Settings

This page describes how dadda? uses cookies and similar technologies on www.daddaexhibition.com. For broader information about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

What we use today

We do not currently use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or optional analytics on this website. Because optional technologies are not in use, there is no cookie consent banner to manage at this time.

The site may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to operate basic functions, maintain security, and remember privacy-related preferences where applicable.

Local storage on your device

Your browser may store small preferences locally using local storage. On this site, that may include your archive grid density preference on the home page and in-progress answers on the submit form until you submit or clear them. These preferences stay on your device and are not used for advertising.

Submission processing

When you submit materials through the form, your information is transmitted to Formspree, our form-processing provider. Formspree may use its own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with that service. Formspree’s practices are governed by its own privacy policy.

Embedded media and future optional technologies

Third-party embedded videos, audio players, social-media features, or other services linked from this site may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies when you interact with them directly.

If we introduce optional analytics, embedded media, or marketing technologies in the future, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy before activating them and will provide a way to manage those preferences where required by law.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Clearing site data may reset local preferences such as grid density or an in-progress submit draft.

Rejecting or deleting cookies will not prevent you from submitting materials to dadda?, though some site preferences may reset.

Questions may be sent to hello@daddaexhibition.com.
