Biography

dadda? is a photo exhibition that centers how single fatherhood is carried, remembered, defended, softened, and misunderstood.

The work photographs fathers alone in spaces built for children. No children are photographed. The absence is the subject.

Opening August 7th · The Find Lab · Provo, Utah

Exhibition

Each dad on the home grid opens into a full project — slides, grid, and story folded together.

Remote interviews and anonymous submissions are accepted through the submit page.
